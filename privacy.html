Ads Optimizer (ADO) POC: privacy policy

Last updated: 06/11/2026

Who we are

ADO is an internal proof-of-concept tool built by First Class Innovation Co., Ltd. (Dr.Pong) to create paused ads for the company's own ad accounts on Meta and TikTok. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app uses

  • IDs of the company's ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets, ads, creatives, Pages and posts.
  • Ad settings the team enters, such as ad names, links and UTM tags.
  • Access tokens issued by Meta or TikTok to the team members who authorise the app.

The app does not collect, store or sell personal data about people who see or interact with the ads, and it does not read private messages.

How the data is stored

During the POC, tokens are kept only on the tester's own computer in the API client's secret storage. They are never published, shared or written to source code. Ad IDs and settings are kept in the team's internal audit log.

Sharing

Data is sent only to Meta and TikTok through their official APIs, to create and read the company's own ads. It is not shared with anyone else.

Deleting your data

To remove the app's access, go to Facebook Settings → Business integrations (or TikTok Business Center → Apps) and remove the app. To ask us to delete anything we hold about you, contact us below and we will delete it within 30 days.
